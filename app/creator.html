Character Creator

Build a Crawl 1st Edition character one step at a time, then play from the finished character sheet.

New character

Everything on the sheet can be typed over, and it saves as you go. Click a star to mark a proficiency and a circle to set a rank. Values worked out from your choices update when you change them in the creator.

Character Creator rules

Classes are edited on the Classes page and backgrounds on the Backgrounds page; races come from the Races page, spells from the Spells page and gear from the Items page. Proficiencies are read from the text: name skills, weapon types, armor and trade skills, write “Either Alchemy or Cooking” for a choice, or “Two skills of your choice” for free picks.

Admin sign-in

One-time setup. Paste a GitHub access key that can change this repo, then choose your admin password. The key is locked with your password before it's stored, so only someone with the password can use it.

Make a key on GitHub: choose only this repository, and set Contents to Read and write.

Use at least 12 characters. A few random words with a number works well.
