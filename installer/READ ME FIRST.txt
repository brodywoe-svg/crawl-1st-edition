CRAWL SPELL COMPENDIUM - WINDOWS APP

1. Right-click this zip and choose "Extract All".
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Install Crawl Spell Compendium.bat".
   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway".
3. The first install downloads the app engine (about 110 MB) from GitHub, so you need internet once.
   After that the app works fully offline.

The app opens by itself when it finishes, and you get Desktop and Start menu shortcuts.
Your spells save automatically on this computer.

To update the app later, run the installer from a newer copy. Your spells are kept.
To remove it, double-click Uninstall.bat.
